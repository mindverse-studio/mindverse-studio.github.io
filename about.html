Music / The proposed platform

Intention into sound.
Listening into feedback.

The proposed loop is Imagine → Interpret → Generate → Listen → Refine. Calibrated BCI features would contribute musical direction, a composer would propose a phrase, and the listener’s subsequent response and direct preferences would guide the next version.

The return channel is the music the person hears. A complete system needs acquisition hardware, personal calibration, signal-quality checks, a conditional composer, and participant evaluation. The sound lab here implements original local synthesis and explicit musical controls.

Ehrlich et al. / PLOS ONE / 2019

A closed-loop, music-based brain-computer interface for emotion mediation

A small non-invasive BCI pilot connected EEG-derived affective feedback to controllable synthesized music. The authors reported preliminary results and substantial participant variation. It provides context for testing a feedback loop, rather than validation of Mindverse Music.

Read the study ↗

Bellier et al. / PLOS Biology / 2023

Music can be reconstructed from human auditory cortex activity using nonlinear decoding models

The study reconstructed aspects of heard music from intracranial recordings. Listening to a known stimulus with intracranial electrodes is a different task from reconstructing an arbitrary imagined melody using scalp EEG. This distinction shapes the research questions for our proposed system.

Read the study ↗

Dance / The proposed experience

Motion to see.
Music to hear.

The proposed Dancing Thought loop is Imagine → Propose → Experience → Respond → Refine. An imagined gesture, form, or character would help direct an audiovisual proposal, with subsequent BCI response patterns and explicit keep, revise, or reject feedback informing refinement.

The motion studio here provides artistic procedural fluid and plasma geometry, original synthesized music, frame export, and browser-based clip rendering. BCI interpretation, learned video generation, and character rigs remain future development.

Li et al. / arXiv / 2026

MindPilot: Closed-loop Visual Stimulation Optimization for Brain Modulation with EEG-guided Diffusion

The authors investigate EEG-guided image generation and closed-loop optimization of visual stimuli. This provides context for studying feedback-guided visual proposals; it does not validate Mindverse’s imagined-dance video system.

Read the research paper ↗

Korik et al. / Frontiers in Neurorobotics / 2019

Decoding Imagined 3D Arm Movement Trajectories From EEG to Control Two Virtual Arms—A Pilot Study

A constrained pilot explored imagined arm trajectories and virtual-arm feedback. Its online-control limitations underline the need for careful calibration and task-specific evaluation. Such control tasks are distinct from reconstructing an arbitrary imagined dance or character.
