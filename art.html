Think Art.
From mind to image.

A research vision for art shaped by imagined ideas, estimated mental states, and your response to what you see. Explore the idea, then create a visual study with the manual controls below.

An idea taking form

Imagination offers direction.
Experience shapes the image.

Think Art explores two connected directions: using calibrated BCI features of an imagined visual intention to guide an artwork, and using estimated mental-state context to shape its palette, energy, and form.

You would view a candidate image, keep what resonates, and guide its next version through explicit feedback and a subsequent BCI response. An image is a creative proposal to explore with the person.

Live BCI interpretation and learned image generation are research directions. The visual studio below uses explicit controls and original procedural graphics.

The proposed BCI loop

Imagine. View.
Respond. Refine.

  1. Imagine — choose a visual direction or a feeling you want to explore.
  2. Interpret — a participant-calibrated model would estimate relevant BCI features, with signal-quality and uncertainty checks.
  3. Create — a generative model would propose an image within that creative direction.
  4. View and respond — your viewing response and direct keep, revise, or reject feedback would inform the next version.
  5. Refine — keep useful elements and change one visual dimension at a time.
