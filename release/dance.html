The proposed creative loop

A video is a proposal.
Your response moves it forward.

Five connected stages for refining a dancing entity through audiovisual experience and feedback.

01 / Imagine

Begin with a movement in mind.

A fluid curl, a rising filament, a character turning to a rhythm. The listener chooses a starting form and imagines the movement they want to explore.

Form + imagined motion→A creative direction

02 / Propose

Give the intention a moving form.

The research vision combines calibrated BCI features with an explicit creative brief to propose a short motion sequence, its visual character, and a background score.

BCI features + context→A candidate video

03 / Experience

Watch the form. Hear its rhythm.

The person watches and listens. Motion, timing, colour, and music create a shared audiovisual experience that they can respond to.

Motion + background music→An audiovisual experience

04 / Respond

Let the response inform the next move.

A future system would evaluate task-specific BCI response patterns alongside direct keep, revise, or reject feedback, using signal-quality and uncertainty checks.

BCI response + preference→A refinement direction

05 / Refine

Keep the character. Evolve the dance.

Revise one dimension at a time: flow, energy, timing, material, or sound. Preserve the versions that resonate and continue exploring the rest.

A chosen direction→The next motion study

A future BCI loop needs participant-specific calibration, reliable response windows, and evaluation against the person’s own stated preferences.
